About this project

Dhatri Biruduraju

Student creator / beginner web developer

I am a student who loves reading, writing, neuroscience, and learning how the brain works. I recently learned HTML and wanted to build something that younger kids could actually use, not just read.

How This Started

My interest in combining neuroscience and technology began through the MAGIC mentoring program, where I worked with my mentor, Somya Panchal. As part of the program, I created my first website about the brain using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. What began as a project to explore something I was interested in eventually became something I wanted to share with others.

Later, I participated in a Brain Bee competition. While preparing for it, I became even more fascinated by the brain. Some topics were difficult at first, but understanding them made me more curious.

That made me wonder: How could I make neuroscience simpler and more fun for younger students?

I decided to take my original project further. I expanded the website, created interactive activities, and began leading workshops where younger students could learn about topics such as memory, focus, sleep, and movement.

That evolution became Journey Through The Brain.

View my original 2024 MAGIC project →

Why I made this

I wanted a website that feels friendly, colorful, and honest. My goal is not to look perfect. My goal is to make neuroscience feel interesting and less scary.

My note

This website is like a science notebook that became a website. I want to keep improving it as I learn more.
