Focus

A workshop about attention, distractions, and how the prefrontal cortex helps us plan, make decisions, control impulses, and stay focused.

Inside this workshop:

  • Distractions and attention
  • The prefrontal cortex
  • Pictionary with distractions
  • Ways to stay focused + reflection

Memory

Kids explore how memories are formed and stored, including sensory, working, and long-term memory and the brain areas that help make remembering possible.

Inside this workshop:

  • Types and stages of memory
  • Hippocampus, prefrontal cortex, and amygdala
  • Neurons, synapses, and neuroplasticity
  • Hands-on memory and interference activities

Sleep

A journey through the stages of sleep and what the brain and body are doing during light sleep, deep sleep, and REM sleep.

Inside this workshop:

  • Light, deep, and REM sleep
  • Sleep spindles and memory
  • How sleep supports the body and brain
  • Memory activity + interactive quizzes

Movement

Kids follow the path from brain to nerves to muscles and learn how different brain areas work together to plan, coordinate, and fine-tune movement.

Inside this workshop:

  • Brain → nerves → muscles
  • Motor cortex
  • Cerebellum and basal ganglia
  • Simon Says, ruler drop, and balance activities
